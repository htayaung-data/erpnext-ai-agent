## 8. Findings by severity

### Blocker before any provider or Python execution

1. **No complete D1 provider exists in the fixed candidate set.** `/usr/bin/bwrap` is absent; `unshare/setpriv` lack sealed-root construction, inherited-FD closure, socket-syscall denial and process/thread denial.
2. **The missing D1 mechanisms have no approved artifact owner.** Seccomp, delegated `pids.max` and sealed-root preparation are only deferred mechanism classes. Any policy/config/helper would exceed the present controller-only source boundary unless separately approved.
3. **C1 cannot be retroactive.** The controller imports file-backed modules at lines 8-12. A same-controller preamble must precede them, while external bootstrap surfaces are prohibited.
4. **No executable control plane exists.** The controller has no supervisor, child runner, provider handshake, result pipe, watchdog, evidence, recovery or teardown boundary.

These Blockers do not prevent this amendment from being ready for Owner choice. They prevent provider selection, source authoring and execution.

### High

1. **Observer placement and independence remain unresolved.** External `strace` before D1 observes pre-denial activity; placing it inside weakens independence; post-denial attachment needs an unauthored race-free handshake.
2. **Raw observer output is leakage-bearing.** Installed evidence states paths and syscall arguments may be printed; raw trace is never promotable.
3. **Teardown is not deterministic.** `timeout`, namespaces, `--kill-child` and `pdeathsig` are partial mechanisms, not a proven process-tree/reaping/recovery composition.
4. **Exact-file loading is not unreachability.** A1 reduces search reach but does not deny direct database-harness reads, credentials, Unix sockets or network/database connections.
5. **Denial cannot self-certify.** A provider receipt without independent O1 evidence cannot support a zero-attempt claim.
6. **Acquisition would create supply-chain and scope risk.** Any absent/new provider requires separately frozen origin, digest, dependency, destination, custody and host-impact authority.

### Medium and deferred

1. Present binary/package provenance is strong but not reproducible-build equivalence.
2. Direct `/usr/bin/python3.10` invocation fields and complete future import origins remain unobserved.
3. Ptrace/Yama/LSM behavior, userns activation and cgroup delegation remain untested.
4. Exit `65/70`, watchdog values, IPC/output limits and every numeric limit remain deferred.
5. VM/container isolation remains outside this parser lane.

### Accounting result

No accounting-semantic Blocker, High or Medium was introduced. Static parser success cannot claim GL/TB correctness, `gl_reconstructed`, company/permission/dimension semantics, E2-B1 acquisition readiness, Finance Cycle 2 closure or accounting authority.
